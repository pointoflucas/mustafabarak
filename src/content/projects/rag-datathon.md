A practical challenge and workshop focused on building RAG systems that work in real-world constraints.

Participants worked on:
- Document ingestion and preprocessing
- Retrieval strategy design
- Prompt engineering
- Evaluation of retrieval quality
- Building end-to-end RAG pipelines

The emphasis was on practical, working systems rather than theoretical perfection.

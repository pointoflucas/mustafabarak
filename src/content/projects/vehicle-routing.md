A practical optimization problem involving shipment allocation, vehicle capacity, routing and real-world operational constraints.

This is a good example of the kind of problem I enjoy: one where algorithmic quality matters, but the mathematically perfect answer isn't necessarily the most useful operational answer.

The work bridged the gap between theoretical optimization and practical operations, considering:
- Real-world capacity constraints
- Operational feasibility
- Cost optimization
- Practical deployment considerations

The goal was finding solutions that were both algorithmically sound and operationally viable.

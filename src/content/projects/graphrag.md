Combining retrieval systems with knowledge graphs and language models to build systems that can answer complex questions over both structured and unstructured information.

This work explored:
- Knowledge graph construction and maintenance
- Integration of graph databases with LLM systems
- Retrieval strategies that leverage both graphs and semantic search
- Building reliable question-answering systems

The goal was moving beyond simple vector-based retrieval to systems that understand relationships and structure in the data.

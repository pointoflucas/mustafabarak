I'm a Data & AI Engineer from Türkiye.

My work has mostly revolved around one broad question: how do we turn
messy information and complicated processes into systems that are
actually useful?

more recently, LLM and agentic AI systems.

I've worked on projects across pharma, retail, finance, venture
capital and optimization-heavy environments. Depending on the
problem, my work has ranged from building ETL pipelines and data
models to designing RAG systems, optimizing distributed processing
and building backend services.

But engineering is only one part of what interests me.

Teaching has become an important part of my professional life.
I've conducted hundreds of technical interviews, mentored engineers,
organized workshops and given talks on topics ranging from Python
and databases to GraphRAG, AI systems and software engineering.

I enjoy situations where technology meets something less technical:
how people make decisions, how teams work, how we learn, and how
tools change our way of thinking.

That's also why this website exists.

It isn't meant to be a polished online resume. It's a place where I
can collect things I've built, ideas I'm exploring, technical notes,
